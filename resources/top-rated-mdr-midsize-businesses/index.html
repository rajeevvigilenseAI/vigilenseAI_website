Why the SOC Must Change for Midsize Businesses

The traditional Security Operations Center (SOC) model is fundamentally broken for the midsize market. Historically, organizations were forced to choose between building an internal team of highly paid analysts or outsourcing to an MDR provider that charged exorbitant per-gigabyte fees. According to the Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report, the vast majority of cyber breaches impact businesses with fewer than 1,000 employees. This statistic highlights a critical vulnerability: attackers know these businesses lack the resources to maintain a 24/7 human-staffed SOC.

Vigilense AI changes the paradigm by replacing the manual labor component of the SOC with high-fidelity, automated AI investigation. This allows for a full-scale SOC workflow that operates continuously. Instead of waiting for a human analyst to wake up and review a backlog of alerts, our AI correlates identity, network, and endpoint data in minutes. This speed is the difference between a minor incident and a catastrophic data breach.

Common Mistakes & Expert Tips

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Over-reliance on EDR alone: Endpoint protection is not enough. You need cross-source correlation to stop sophisticated attacks.
  • Ignoring "Low" alerts: Attackers often use low-level noise to mask their primary intrusion. AI triage ensures these are never missed.
  • Scaling with costs: Choosing a provider that charges by the gigabyte leads to "security blindness" as your data grows.

Expert tips for midsize security

  • Prioritize Identity: Most breaches involve stolen credentials. Ensure your MDR monitors identity logs closely.
  • Automate everything: If a task can be handled by AI, don't waste human hours on it. Save your team for high-level strategy.
  • Test your response: Regularly simulate incidents to ensure your team knows how to react when the AI flags a threat.
