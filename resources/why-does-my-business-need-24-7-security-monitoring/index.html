In today's hyper-connected digital landscape, the "9-to-5" approach to cybersecurity is no longer a viable defense strategy. Cybercriminals utilize automated tools that scan for vulnerabilities 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. For midsize organizations, the assumption that "we are too small to be targeted" is a dangerous misconception. In reality, smaller businesses are often viewed as "soft targets" because they lack the robust security operations centers (SOCs) found in larger enterprises. Vigilense AI bridges this gap, providing your organization with the same level of sophisticated threat detection and rapid incident response previously reserved for Fortune 500 companies, all without requiring a dedicated internal security team.

The Hidden Dangers of "Office Hour" Security

Many business leaders believe that their firewall and basic antivirus software are sufficient. However, these tools are static defenses. Modern cyberattacks, such as ransomware and business email compromise (BEC), are dynamic and persistent. When an attacker gains access to your network on a Friday night, they have nearly 60 hours of unmonitored time to navigate your systems, escalate privileges, and prepare their payload before your team returns on Monday.

By the time your staff discovers the anomaly, the damage is often irreversible. 24/7 monitoring acts as your digital sentry, identifying unusual patterns - such as mass file access or unauthorized logins - at any time of the day or night. Vigilense AI automates this triage, ensuring that genuine threats are flagged and addressed immediately, while reducing "alert fatigue" for your IT staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the biggest risk of not having 24/7 monitoring?

The primary risk is "dwell time." Attackers can remain inside your network for weeks or months, harvesting data or planting ransomware. Without 24/7 monitoring, you have no eyes on your network during nights, weekends, and holidays, which is exactly when attackers prefer to launch their most damaging campaigns.

How does Vigilense AI avoid data ingestion fees?

Most MDR providers charge per gigabyte for log ingestion because they move your data into their own cloud. Vigilense AI uses a proprietary query-in-place technology. We bring the security intelligence to your data, rather than bringing your data to our security platform, eliminating all ingestion costs.

Can AI really replace a human security team?

AI excels at the high-speed, repetitive task of triaging thousands of alerts. By handling the "heavy lifting," our AI allows your existing IT team to focus on high-level strategy. We maintain a "human-in-the-loop" model, meaning when a complex threat requires a nuanced decision, our team or your experts are looped in instantly.
