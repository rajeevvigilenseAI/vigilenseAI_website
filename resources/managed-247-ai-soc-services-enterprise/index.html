Common Mistakes & Expert Tips

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-relying on manual processes: Trying to handle every alert manually leads to burnout and missed threats.
  • Ignoring data hygiene: If your input data is poor, your security outcomes will be poor. Clean your logs regularly.
  • Cloud-locking: Avoid vendors who force you into their proprietary clouds; it limits your long-term flexibility.
  • Treating security as a one-time project: Cyber threats are constant; your defense strategy must be equally persistent.

Expert Tips for Success

  • Prioritize Visibility: You cannot defend what you cannot see. Ensure your endpoints, network, and identity providers are all visible to your AI.
  • Focus on Triage: The goal is to reduce "noise." Use AI to filter out false positives so your team focuses on real risks.
  • Automate Response: Where possible, automate the isolation of compromised assets to limit the blast radius of an attack.
  • Think Strategically: Use the time saved by automation to focus on architecture and long-term security roadmaps.

What data sources does Vigilense monitor?

Vigilense AI correlates alerts across a wide array of sources, including endpoints, identity management systems, network traffic, and threat intelligence feeds. By synthesizing data from over 50 different sources, our AI is able to detect patterns and anomalies that would be impossible for a human analyst or a simple rule-based system to identify. This multi-layered approach ensures that even if an attacker manages to bypass one layer of your security, our AI will detect the subsequent steps of the attack chain, providing you with early warning and the capability to respond before damage occurs.

How does the AI handle false positives?

False positives are the "noise" that plagues traditional security systems, leading to alert fatigue and missed incidents. Vigilense AI is specifically engineered to minimize this noise through advanced correlation. Before an alert ever reaches your team, our AI investigates the context, correlating it against known threat patterns, user behavior, and historical data. If the AI determines an event is benign, it suppresses the alert automatically. This ensures that when your team does receive a notification, it is a high-fidelity, verified threat that requires immediate action, saving you time and reducing stress.

Can I maintain control over my own data?

Yes. Data sovereignty is a core principle at Vigilense AI. Unlike many MDR providers who require you to shift all your data into their proprietary cloud - effectively locking you in - our architecture is designed to work with your existing environment. We analyze your data where it lives, ensuring you maintain ownership and control. This approach not only complies with strict data privacy regulations but also provides you with greater architectural flexibility, allowing you to move or change your infrastructure without needing to worry about the security vendor's restrictive cloud requirements.

What happens during a security incident?

When our AI detects a verified threat, the system immediately flags it for investigation and provides your team with the full context of the incident. This includes a breakdown of the alert, the sources involved, and the potential impact. Because the AI has already done the "heavy lifting" of investigation, your team is presented with a clear picture of what happened, why it matters, and the recommended steps for remediation. This allows your team to respond decisively, minimizing downtime and preventing the attacker from causing further harm to your systems or data.
