SMS Terms

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

These SMS Terms govern the Vigilense AI text messaging program ("Vigilense AI Alerts" or the "Program"), operated by Vigilense AI ("Vigilense AI," "we," "us," or "our"). By opting in to receive text messages from us, you agree to these SMS Terms, our Terms of Service, and our Privacy Policy.

Quick reference: Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email support@vigilense.ai.

1. Program Description

Vigilense AI Alerts sends text messages to customers, prospective customers, and authorized users of the Vigilense AI platform. Depending on what you opt in to, you may receive:

  • Security alert notifications: notices about threats detected in your environment, incident status updates, and requests to approve containment actions
  • Demo and meeting messages: confirmations and reminders for demos or calls you have booked with us
  • Account messages: one-time passcodes for signing in, and important notices about your account
  • Marketing messages: product updates, event invitations, and promotional offers, only if you separately opt in to marketing messages

2. How You Opt In

You will only receive text messages from us if you have given consent. You can opt in by:

  • Entering your mobile number and checking the SMS consent box on a form on our website, such as the demo request form
  • Turning on SMS notifications for your account in the Vigilense AI platform
  • Giving your consent to us in writing, for example as part of your onboarding with us

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You must be the account holder or an authorized user of the mobile number you provide, and at least 18 years of age.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Security alert notifications depend on activity in your environment and your notification settings. Demo and meeting messages are sent around the meetings you book. Account messages are sent when you sign in or when there is an important change to your account.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Vigilense AI does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier's standard messaging and data rates apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

5. How to Opt Out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message from us (you can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT). You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will not receive further text messages from us unless you opt in again.

You can also opt out by emailing support@vigilense.ai or, for platform users, by turning off SMS notifications in your account settings.

If you opt out of SMS security alerts, you will continue to receive them through your other notification channels, such as email and the Vigilense AI platform.

6. How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any message from us for help, or contact us at support@vigilense.ai.

7. Delivery

Supported carriers include all major U.S. mobile carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery of text messages is not guaranteed and may be affected by factors outside our control, so SMS should not be your only channel for time-critical security notifications.

8. Privacy

We use your mobile number only to operate the Program and as described in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages and provide customer support.

9. Changes to These SMS Terms

We may update these SMS Terms from time to time. We will post the updated terms on this page and update the "Last Updated" date above. Continuing to receive messages after a change means you accept the updated terms.
