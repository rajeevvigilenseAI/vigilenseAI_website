<h2>The Hidden Dangers of "Office Hour" Security</h2>
<p>Many business leaders believe that their firewall and basic antivirus software are sufficient. However, these tools are static defenses. Modern cyberattacks, such as ransomware and business email compromise (BEC), are dynamic and persistent. When an attacker gains access to your network on a Friday night, they have nearly 60 hours of unmonitored time to navigate your systems, escalate privileges, and prepare their payload before your team returns on Monday.</p>
<p>By the time your staff discovers the anomaly, the damage is often irreversible. 24/7 monitoring acts as your digital sentry, identifying unusual patterns—such as mass file access or unauthorized logins—at any time of the day or night. Vigilense AI automates this triage, ensuring that genuine threats are flagged and addressed immediately, while reducing "alert fatigue" for your IT staff.</p>
