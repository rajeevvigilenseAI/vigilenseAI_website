<h2>The Financial Case Against Traditional MDR Models</h2>
<p>When evaluating MDR providers with no ingestion fees, the primary driver is often cost predictability. Traditional MDR vendors operate on a model that scales linearly with your data volume. As your organization matures and your logging becomes more granular, your security bill spikes. This creates a perverse incentive: the more security data you collect to protect your business, the more you are penalized by your vendor.</p>
<p>Vigilense AI removes this friction. By utilizing a fixed-fee model based on your infrastructure rather than your log volume, we allow you to scale your security visibility without fear of budget overruns. <h2>How to Select the Right Managed Detection Service (7 Steps)</h2>
<p>Step 1: Audit your current data storage. Before choosing an MDR, identify where your logs reside. If you are already using cloud-native data lakes, prioritize providers that can query these directly.</p>
<p>Step 2: Assess your compliance requirements. Ensure the MDR provider can offer a complete audit trail of every automated action taken by their AI or SOC teams.</p>
<p>Step 3: Evaluate the "Human-in-the-Loop" aspect. AI is powerful, but complex breaches often require human context. Choose a partner that blends automation with expert oversight.</p>
<p>Step 4: Request a transparent pricing model. Avoid providers that hide costs behind complex "per-GB" calculations that become opaque as your environment grows.</p>
<p>Step 5: Test the integration timeline. A good MDR provider should be able to demonstrate a proof-of-concept in your environment within a few business days.</p>
<p>Step 6: Review detection coverage. Does the provider correlate data across identity, network, and endpoints? Siloed monitoring is a vulnerability waiting to happen.</p>
<p>Step 7: Check for vendor lock-in terms. Ensure you can migrate or change services without losing your historical security data or being trapped by proprietary formats.</p>
</section>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Why do most MDR providers charge ingestion fees?</h3>
<p>Traditional providers charge ingestion fees because they host your data in their own private cloud. They incur storage and compute costs for every gigabyte of log data you send them. By moving your data into their environment, they create a "walled garden" that makes it difficult for you to leave, while also creating a high-margin revenue stream based on your data volume. Vigilense AI changes this by querying your existing data, removing the need for us to store your logs ourselves.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-item">
<h3>Is my data secure if it stays in my infrastructure?</h3>
<p>Yes, in many ways it is more secure. When you store data in your own infrastructure (like an S3 bucket or Snowflake instance), you retain full control over access policies, encryption keys, and lifecycle management. Vigilense AI only queries the data required for threat detection and response. We do not require you to hand over ownership of your logs or expose them to unauthorized third-party environments. This model adheres to the principle of least privilege, minimizing the attack surface associated with log management.</p>
