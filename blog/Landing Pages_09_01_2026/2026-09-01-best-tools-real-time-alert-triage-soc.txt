<p>The best tools for real-time alert triage bridge this gap by focusing on efficiency and automation. <h2>Expert Tips for Security Triage</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eliminate Noise First:</strong> Use AI to suppress known-good traffic. If an alert isn't actionable, it shouldn't be interrupting your team.</li>
<li><strong>Context is King:</strong> Always prioritize tools that link identity to endpoint activity. A password reset followed by a suspicious login is a clear indicator of compromise.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Mean Time to Respond (MTTR):</strong> The goal of triage isn't just to see the threat, but to reduce the time it takes to neutralize it.</li>
</ul>

<section>
<h2>Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>
<p>One of the biggest mistakes organizations make is over-investing in tools they don't have the staff to operate. A powerful SIEM is useless if it simply sits idle because no one has the time to configure it. Another common error is failing to account for data ingestion costs; many companies are surprised to find that their "affordable" security tool becomes prohibitively expensive as their logs grow. Finally, many businesses rely on point-in-time scanning rather than continuous, real-time monitoring, leaving them vulnerable to breaches that occur between scans.</p>
