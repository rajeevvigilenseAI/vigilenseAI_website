<h2>Common Mistakes &amp; Expert Tips</h2>
<h3>Common mistakes to avoid</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Over-reliance on EDR alone:</strong> Endpoint protection is not enough. You need cross-source correlation to stop sophisticated attacks.</li>
<li><strong>Ignoring "Low" alerts:</strong> Attackers often use low-level noise to mask their primary intrusion. AI triage ensures these are never missed.</li>
<li><strong>Scaling with costs:</strong> Choosing a provider that charges by the gigabyte leads to "security blindness" as your data grows.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Expert tips for midsize security</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Prioritize Identity:</strong> Most breaches involve stolen credentials. Ensure your MDR monitors identity logs closely.</li>
<li><strong>Automate everything:</strong> If a task can be handled by AI, don't waste human hours on it. Save your team for high-level strategy.</li>
<li><strong>Test your response:</strong> Regularly simulate incidents to ensure your team knows how to react when the AI flags a threat.</li>
</ul>
